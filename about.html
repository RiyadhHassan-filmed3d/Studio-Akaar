About us

Space, shaped by clarity.

Studio Akaar is an architectural studio focused on solving spatial challenges through functional clarity and systematic design thinking. We combine contemporary strategies with the discipline and wisdom embedded in architectural tradition.

Every design decision responds to climate, materiality, budget and human comfort. Bangladesh's tropical climate demands thoughtful design — we analyse solar paths, wind patterns, rainfall and cultural context so spaces perform as beautifully as they look.

Architecture & design services in Dhaka

What we design in Dhaka.

Architecture & exterior design

Contemporary homes, apartment buildings, mosques and institutional buildings with facades shaped for Bangladesh's tropical climate — sun, rain, wind and ventilation.

Interior design & home decoration

Complete interiors and renovations for homes and apartments: spatial planning, colour, raw materials, lighting, furniture and joinery.

Commercial, restaurant & showroom interiors

Offices, restaurants, fitness studios and brand showrooms designed to work hard every day and to express the identity of the brand.

Landscape architecture

Residential gardens, public spaces and large-scale masterplans that connect buildings with greenery, water and open space.

3D visualisation & rendering

Realistic 3D models, renders and walkthroughs so you can see and approve the design before construction begins.

Project management & supervision

BOQ and documentation, site supervision and handover — one team from the first hand sketch to the finished space.
